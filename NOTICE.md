# Third-party data notices

Mikiri Trails uses data from the following sources. Each keeps its own licence.

## OpenStreetMap

Trail routes, trailheads and other map data are derived from OpenStreetMap.

© OpenStreetMap contributors. The data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL)
1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/ — see https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.

Databases produced from OpenStreetMap data (such as the trail routes stored by this project)
remain subject to the ODbL when they are publicly used or shared.

## Wikimedia Commons

Trail photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under the licence their authors chose
for each file (for example CC BY or CC BY-SA). The author, licence and source of every photo
are listed in [`content/CREDITS.md`](content/CREDITS.md), stored with the photo
(`trail_media.caption`, formatted `Photo: <author> · <license> · Wikimedia Commons`), and
shipped in the app (`mobile/assets/data/photo-credits.json`).

## NASA SRTM

Elevation data comes from the NASA Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM), distributed by
NASA and the U.S. Geological Survey.
